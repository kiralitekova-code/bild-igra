# Design Lenses

Use these prompts when refining mechanics, player feel, accessibility, or design gaps.

## Mechanics, Dynamics, and Player Experience

1. Mechanics
   - What rules, verbs, resources, constraints, and feedback define play?
   - Which mechanics are mandatory for the core loop?
   - Which mechanics are optional, risky, or out of scope?
2. Dynamics
   - What player behaviors should emerge from those mechanics?
   - What dominant strategies, exploits, or frustration loops might appear?
   - How does difficulty change as the player gains mastery?
3. Player Experience
   - What should the player feel: tension, mastery, curiosity, speed, comfort, surprise, expression, or competition?
   - Which feedback proves that the intended feeling is happening?
   - Which parts of the design fight the intended feeling?
4. Iteration
   - Convert vague feedback into a rule, tuning, level, UI, audio, or onboarding change.
   - Record the smallest test that can confirm the change.

## Accessibility Prompts

1. Controls
   - Support remapping, alternate inputs, hold/toggle options, and adjustable timing where the design allows.
   - Avoid requiring rapid repeated input unless it is central to the challenge.
2. Readability
   - Check text size, contrast, color reliance, icon clarity, motion, camera shake, and visual noise.
   - Provide redundant cues for critical information.
3. Audio Alternatives
   - Capture subtitle, caption, visual alert, and volume-mix needs early.
   - Do not make audio-only cues mandatory without an alternate path.
4. Difficulty and Assists
   - Identify adjustable challenge, hint, checkpoint, speed, damage, aim, or fail-state options.
   - Preserve the intended experience while giving players usable control.
5. Cognitive Load
   - Limit simultaneous objectives, unexplained symbols, hidden timers, and memory-heavy instructions.
   - Document onboarding, reminders, pause-state access, and objective clarity.
6. Validation
   - Add accessibility checks to playtest goals instead of treating them as a late polish pass.
